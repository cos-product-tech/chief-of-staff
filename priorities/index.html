P3 · Medium
07
Make the architecture half of the Engineering Operating System real
▾Summary
Partner with Sim to make the architecture half of the Engineering Operating System real: the system that connects every Principal and Architect, so it is practiced, not just published. The operating principles are set: teams own their design within agreed standards, with a short decision record for significant cross-domain choices; build on what we have, gap analysis first, extracting shared services as part of new work; shared patterns (APIs and events, identity, ledger, observability, cloud, data contracts) with mandatory approval only for defined security, regulatory, and material exceptions. The Architecture Chapter is the vehicle: a practice and advisory community sponsored by the CTO, architects and principal engineers as core members, open to Platform, SRE, Security, Data, and Product, with a facilitator to be named. It is not an approval queue. This is the scope of the future senior architecture leadership role, available for Sim to demonstrate now; demonstrated first, titled after.
▾Objective and key results
Objective
Architecture is practiced, not just published: teams own their design within shared patterns, significant cross-domain decisions are recorded, and the Architecture Chapter advises early instead of approving late.
Key results
Architecture Chapter chartered with a named facilitator and core members, meeting on cadence, with Platform, SRE, Security, Data, and Product participating, by December 31, 2026Every significant cross-domain decision has a short decision record before build starts; mandatory approval limited to defined security, regulatory, and material exceptionsShared patterns published and in use for APIs and events, identity, ledger, observability, cloud, and data contractsSim leads the Chapter and demonstrates the senior architecture leadership scope by December 31, 2026
