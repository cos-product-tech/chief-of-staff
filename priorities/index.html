Partner with Sim to make the architecture half of the Engineering Operating System real: the system that connects every Principal and Architect, so it is practiced, not just published. It is not an approval queue. This is the scope of the future senior architecture leadership role, available for Sim to demonstrate now; demonstrated first, titled after.
