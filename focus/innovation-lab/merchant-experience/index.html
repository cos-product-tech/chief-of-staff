What we'll diagnose first Discovery · the five questions behind every survey

01

Where the time goes

How long it takes to move from a business request to production, and where time is spent waiting or reworking along the way.

02

Whether work is ready to commit

Whether we have a clear customer or business problem, a hypothesis tied to a measurable KPI, early collaboration across Product, Design, Engineering, and QA, and delivery dates grounded in actual capacity and dependencies.

03

Where delivery gets stuck

How much time goes into building versus waiting on reviews, QA and regression, environments, decisions, or other teams, and how often new work is added without explicit trade-offs.

04

Whether we deliver incrementally and learn

Whether we break work into smaller, valuable releases and measure the impact after launch to determine what to do next.

05

How AI changes the work

Where each role uses AI today, and whether it improves delivery speed, quality, or both.

Discovery surveys Phase 1 · Oct 13 – Oct 23 · one five-question survey per role · Google Forms, about 10 minutes · synthesized without attribution

How it runs
  • Survey distribution. Eric sends each participant the survey for their role by end of day Wednesday, October 21.
  • Responses due. Participants complete the surveys by October 23. Responses are collected with respondent email addresses so the Tiger Team can follow up as needed.
  • Review and synthesis. Each Tiger Team member reviews and synthesizes responses for their assigned functions and determines how to partner with those teams: Bart Product Management and Product Design · Eric Engineering Management and Technical Program Management · Amrita Software Engineering · Steve QA Engineering.
  • Built for speed, not perfection. The surveys were written to get signal within days, not to capture every question each Tiger Team member would have asked. Circulating every question for review would have added weeks and risked analysis paralysis. Where a survey does not cover something a Tiger Team member needs to know, they follow up with that person directly during Phase 2.

Future scope once the cycle is faster · not held to in this window

Every new commitment states a hypothesis. We believe shipping this for this customer will move this KPI from its value today to a target, because of this reason. Without the hypothesis it is a feature; with it, it is a bet the team can learn from.
The team owns its KPIs. They sit on the team's own dashboard, the team looks at them every sprint, and for every shipped commitment the team can say whether the KPI moved and what it will do next.
