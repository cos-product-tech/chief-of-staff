The line five changes, one Sprint 24 to try them, four sprints to measure them · click a tile to open it

The team is not short on craft. Engineers own their quality, AI has already made the coding fast, and the team's own retro fixes are small and specific. What is slow is everything around the code: work arrives without a why, the sprint is planned without a capacity number, cross-team decisions have no owner, releases are all-or-nothing, and nobody reads whether a release did anything.

So the five above the line are about the intake and the plan, not the engineers. They are ordered by which number they move first, whether they fit a two-week pairing sprint, and whether the team can own them without a decision from outside. Each one depends on the one before it.

01

Nothing enters the sprint unready.

A story is ready when it carries the why (problem, outcome, KPI), acceptance criteria, an estimate, its QA task, its test-data need, and the design, with any other team named. Planning plans; it no longer grooms.

Why this order the three tests every item was read against

Which number does it move first?

Sprint addition rate (263%) and predictability (18.8%) are the worst on the board and the most sensitive to intake and planning. 01 and 02 aim at them directly. Cycle time (5.82 days) follows once work stops being discovered mid-ticket and stops waiting in QA.

Does it fit the window?

Sprint 24 is two weeks with Thanksgiving inside it, then four sprints across the holidays. Anything above the line has to be tried in pairing and visible in Jellyfish by Feb 16. Environments, the DIP, and a second PM cannot; they are escalated, not dropped.

Can the team own it?

The five need Erin, Alex, Kyle, Quan, and the engineers, with the Tiger Team pairing. Nothing above the line waits on a budget, a vendor, or an org decision. The plan said three or four changes; the fifth is on the list because it costs thirty minutes and it is the only one about value rather than throughput.

How this page changes. The order is a proposal until Nov 20, when the team chooses with us. Probe 04 can move 06 above the line; the Dec 18 checkpoint can move anything. Each card links to the survey, conversation, or ritual it came from, so the argument can be checked, not just read.
